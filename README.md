# My Favorite Series: **Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)**

![Money Heist](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/8/8a/Money_Heist.jpg)

## **Overview**
**Money Heist** is a Spanish crime drama series that follows a group of people led by a mastermind known as **The Professor**. 
Their goal is to pull off the **biggest heists in history**, starting with the Royal Mint of Spain.



## **Why I Like It**
- The storyline is **intense** and full of unexpected twists.
- The character development is amazing.
- The theme song *Bella Ciao* became iconic.
- It shows teamwork, loyalty, and strategy.



## **Main Characters**
| Character Code Name | Role | Personality |
|--------------------|------|-------------|
| **The Professor**  | Mastermind | Calm and genius planner |
| **Tokyo**          | Narrator/Robber | Emotional but brave |
| **Berlin**         | Leader inside the heist | Confident and strict |
| **Rio**            | Hacker | Soft-hearted and loyal |



## **Favorite Quote**
> *"In this world, everything is governed by balance. There's what you stand to gain and what you stand to lose. And when you think you've got nothing to lose, you become overconfident."*  
> — **The Professor**
